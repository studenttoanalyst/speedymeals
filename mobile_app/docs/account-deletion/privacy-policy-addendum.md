## 9. Data Deletion

### 9.1 How to request deletion

You can ask us to delete your SpeedyMeals account and your personal data in either of two ways:

1. **In the app** — open your profile and choose *Delete account* (riders: *Account → Request Account Deletion*).
2. **On the web, without signing in** — visit **https://speedymealservices.com/delete-account**, enter your registered phone number and confirm the verification code we send you by SMS.

You can also email **support@speedymealservices.com** from the number or email address you registered with.

### 9.2 What happens to a customer account

If you have **no active or pending order**, your account and personal data are deleted **immediately** when your request is verified: your name, phone number, email and saved addresses are removed. If you still have an active or pending order, we will ask you to wait until it is delivered or cancelled — we answer with a clear message and retry then.

### 9.3 What happens to a rider account (7-day grace period, admin review)

Rider accounts are **never deleted automatically**. A deletion request works like this:

1. **Immediately:** your rider account is deactivated — you stop receiving new delivery offers. Your request is recorded with the date.
2. **After 7 days (grace period):** your request moves into admin review. You can still cancel your request at any time before it is approved, and your account becomes active again.
3. **Admin review:** a staff member decides on your request. We aim to complete this review **within 30 days** of your request.
4. **Deletion only after approval, and only with no pending dues:** your account can be deleted only if your **wallet balance is Rs. 0** and any **cash you have collected on COD deliveries (pending cash owed) is Rs. 0**, and you have **no active delivery**. These dues must be settled first — they can never be waived by an approval. If dues or a delivery appear while your request is waiting, your request is placed *on hold* with the reason and we will contact you.
5. **What is removed:** your name, phone number, CNIC number, vehicle details and your last known location are anonymised or cleared, your sign-in sessions and app push registrations are revoked, and any saved addresses are deleted.
6. **What is kept:** your orders, deliveries, wallet transactions, payouts and cash deposits are kept in **anonymised form** (they are no longer linked to an identified person). We keep them because accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution and legal-compliance rules require it, and only for as long as necessary.

### 9.4 Rider KYC documents (CNIC, driving licence, vehicle photos)

Your uploaded documents are stored privately. They are never published and never returned through any customer- or rider-facing feature. At the moment your deletion request is approved, the reviewing admin chooses one of:

- **delete now** — the files are removed from storage and the references to them are deleted from our database straight away, or
- **retain until a date** — the files are kept privately until a later date set by the admin, and are then removed manually by our staff. Until then they remain private and inaccessible to other users.

Your KYC documents are therefore never kept indefinitely by accident: each is either deleted on approval or purged on a set date.

### 9.5 Cancellation and questions

While your request is still *pending* or *under review* you can cancel it, in the app or on the deletion page, and your account is reactivated. Once an admin has approved your request, it can no longer be cancelled.

Questions about deletion: **support@speedymealservices.com**.
