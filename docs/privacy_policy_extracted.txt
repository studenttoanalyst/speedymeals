3. Data We Collect
3.1 Data you give us on the Website
When you submit the registration form, we collect the information the form asks for. This is currently:
your name;
your mobile number;
your city or area;
your role (Customer, Rider or Restaurant Partner) and, for a Restaurant Partner, your business details such as the business name; and
any message or information you choose to send us.
3.2 Data collected automatically on the Website
When you visit or use the Website, our systems may record basic technical data needed to run and protect it, such as:
IP address, browser type and version, device type and operating system;
pages visited and date and time of access;
security and error logs.
3.3 Records of your consent and communications
We keep a record that you accepted our Terms and this Policy, including the version accepted and the date and time. We also keep messages you send us and our replies (for example, support emails).
3.4 What the App collects
The App does not collect, store or transmit your personal data. It does not use analytics or advertising tools. It requests only the permission to access the internet, so that it can open the Website in your browser. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or storage.
When you tap a registration or Terms/Privacy link in the App, your phone's browser opens the Website. From that point, the Website's handling of data applies.
3.5 What we do not collect now
At this stage we do not collect: identity documents (CNIC, licence), photos, precise location, payment or bank details, or contact lists.

5. Who We Share Data With
We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these cases:
Service providers who help us run the Website and our operations, for example hosting and infrastructure, database, messaging or email providers. They may process data only on our instructions and to provide their service to us.
Legal and safety reasons, where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a regulator or law-enforcement body, or to protect our rights, our users or the public from fraud or harm.
Business changes, if Speedy Meals is merged with, or its business is transferred to, another entity. We will make sure your data stays protected and will tell you of material changes.
With your consent, where you ask us to share your data.
Our staff can access your data only where they need it for the purposes above.
6. Contacting You and Marketing
We may contact you by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email for onboarding and launch updates that relate to your Registration.
We send marketing or promotional messages only if you opt in. You may opt out at any time by replying "STOP" (or the equivalent) or by emailing support@speedymealservices.com.
Opting out of marketing does not stop service messages that we need to send (such as a launch or account notice relating to your Registration).

8. Your Rights and Choices
You may ask us to:
access the personal data we hold about you;
correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
delete your data (see Section 9);
withdraw your Registration or your consent to contact;
opt out of marketing at any time.
To exercise these rights, email support@speedymealservices.com from the email address or phone number you registered with, or tell us the details you used so we can verify it is you. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days. If we cannot fully act on a request (for example, where the law requires us to keep some data), we will explain why.
9. Data Deletion
You can ask us to delete your data and withdraw your Registration at any time.
How to request deletion
Send an email to support@speedymealservices.com with the subject "Delete my data".
Include the name and mobile number you used to register.
We will confirm your identity and then process your request.
What happens
We delete or anonymise your registration data and any account data we hold about you.
We may keep limited data where we must or need to, for fraud prevention, security, resolving disputes, tax or accounting, or legal compliance. We keep it only for as long as necessary.
We will confirm to you once your request has been completed.
Deleting your data ends your Registration. You can register again later if you wish.
10. How We Protect Your Data
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:
encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your device and the Website;
access to personal data limited to authorised people who need it;
security logging and monitoring for abuse and fake registrations;
secure storage with our providers.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects you and the law requires it, we will notify you and the relevant authority.
11. Cookies and Similar Technologies
The Website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for it to work and stay secure (for example, to remember your form input or protect against abuse). If we later add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this Policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.
You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect how the Website works.
12. Children
Speedy Meals is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, contact support@speedymealservices.com and we will delete the data.
13. Storage Location and Transfers Outside Pakistan
Our service providers may store or process data on servers located outside Pakistan. Where we transfer data, we take reasonable steps to make sure it stays protected in line with this Policy and applicable law.
