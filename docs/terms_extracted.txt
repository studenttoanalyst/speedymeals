D2.3 Before going online for the first time, a Rider must complete the Wallet recharge (Clause D3) and pay the Kit Deposit (Clause D5).
D3. Prepaid Wallet
D3.1 First recharge. A Rider must make a minimum first recharge of Rs. 500.
D3.2 Going online. A Rider may go online only while the Wallet balance is at least Rs. 100.
D3.3 Per-delivery fee. Rs. 10 is automatically deducted from the Wallet when the Rider marks an Order "Delivered". This fee is non-refundable.
D3.4 Low balance. If the balance falls below Rs. 100, the Rider is taken offline and prompted to recharge. A delivery already in progress must still be completed.
D3.5 Recharge methods. Bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa or card.
D3.6 Closure. On account closure, the remaining Wallet balance is refunded within seven (7) working days, after deduction of any amounts the Rider owes Speedy Meals.
D3.7 The Wallet balance is a prepayment for services. It is not a bank deposit and does not earn interest.
D4. Delivery Fee Earnings and Payout
D4.1 A Rider receives 100% of the Delivery Fee for each completed delivery. Speedy Meals takes no share of it.
D4.2 Earnings are paid weekly by bank or mobile wallet transfer, separately from any cash the Rider deposits.
D4.3 Speedy Meals may set off amounts the Rider owes (including undeposited cash or unpaid dues) against earnings, to the extent permitted by law.
D5. Equipment and Kit Deposit
D5.1 Speedy Meals issues a branded uniform and delivery bag against a refundable with in 7 working days  Kit Deposit of Rs. 4,000. The Kit Deposit is separate from the Wallet.
D5.2 The Kit Deposit is refunded within seven (7) working days after all of the following: (a) the Rider's account is closed, (b) the equipment is returned in acceptable condition, and (c) all amounts owed by the Rider are settled.
D5.3 Speedy Meals may deduct from the Kit Deposit: the cost of damaged, lost or unreturned equipment; and, where the Rider fails to deposit collected COD cash, the unpaid amount, to the extent permitted by law. The Rider will be informed of any deduction and its reason.
D5.4 Riders must use the equipment only for Speedy Meals deliveries and must not sell, transfer or misuse it.
D6. Accepting and Completing Deliveries
D6.1 Requests show pickup and drop-off locations, payment type, distance and the Delivery Fee earning. A Rider may accept or reject each request.
D6.2 Once a request is accepted, the Rider is expected to pick up and deliver promptly and professionally, to handle food carefully, and not to open or tamper with any bag or seal. On the doubt bases rider may open the parcel.
D6.3 The Rider must not exchange any payment with the Restaurant Partner at any time, whatever the Order's payment method.
D6.4 The Rider must update Order status accurately in the app (arrived, picked up, on the way, delivered). Location is shared while the Rider is online.
D6.5 Where the Customer is unreachable, the Rider must wait up to ten (10) minutes, attempt contact and record the attempt in the app before marking the Order failed.
D7. Cash on Delivery Handling
D7.1 The Rider collects the full Order Total (Food Subtotal plus Delivery Fee) in cash from the Customer. Collected cash is Speedy Meals' money held in trust, not the Rider's own money.
D7.2 Daily deposit. The Rider must deposit all Pending Cash by the end of each day, through a Speedy Meals outlet or hub, bank transfer to the Speedy Meals company account, or an approved mobile wallet. The Rider must submit the amount and payment reference or proof in the app.
D7.3 Verification. Outlet deposits are confirmed by Speedy Meals. For bank or wallet transfers, Speedy Meals may restore COD access on a provisional basis pending verification. Submitting false proof results in suspension.
D7.4 Cash limit. When Pending Cash reaches Rs. 4,000, the app warns the Rider to deposit. When Pending Cash reaches Rs. 5,000, no new COD Orders are assigned until the Rider deposits. Digital Payment Orders continue to be assigned.
D7.5 Next-day rule. A Rider with undeposited Pending Cash from a previous day cannot go online until it is deposited.
D7.6 Reconciliation. Speedy Meals compares expected cash from completed COD Orders with deposits. Discrepancies are investigated. A Rider must explain and repay any shortfall.
D7.7 Failure to deposit cash is a material breach. It may lead to immediate suspension, deduction under Clauses D4.3 and D5.3, blacklisting, and civil or criminal action (including a police report using the identity documents provided at verification).
D8. Conduct, Suspension and Termination
D8.1 Speedy Meals may suspend or terminate a Rider for fraud, cash shortfalls, forged documents, tampering with food, harassment, unsafe or illegal conduct, or repeated breaches.
D8.2 A Rider found engaging in illegal activity will be permanently terminated, blacklisted and reported to law enforcement. Speedy Meals is not liable for any unlawful act or traffic violation by a Rider (subject to Clause A7.3).
D8.3 A Rider may close the account at any time. Refunds under Clauses D3.6 and D5.2 apply after dues are settled.

E3.3 No upfront deposit or registration fee is charged.

E4.1 Speedy Meals pays the Restaurant Partner directly, weekly, by bank transfer. The Net Payable is 90% of the Food Subtotal of eligible Orders (after commission). This applies whether the Customer paid by COD or Digital Payment. A Rider never pays a Restaurant Partner.
E4.2 Settlement is made each Monday for Orders completed in the previous Monday-to-Sunday week. If Monday is a public holiday, payment is made on the next working day.
E4.3 The dashboard shows Total Sales, Commission Deducted and Net Payable for each week.
E4.4 Disputes. The Restaurant Partner must raise any settlement dispute within seven (7) days of the settlement statement. After that the statement is treated as accepted.
E4.5 Amounts for Orders refunded because of the Restaurant Partner's fault (Schedule 2), and any agreed adjustments, are deducted from settlement. No commission is charged on cancelled Orders.
E4.6 The Restaurant Partner is responsible for its own taxes. Speedy Meals may deduct or withhold taxes where required by law.

Speedy Meals may suspend or deactivate a Restaurant Partner for repeated late or rejected Orders, food safety violations or complaints, false information, circumvention of the commission system, or breach of these Terms. Amounts due for completed Orders remain payable, subject to lawful deductions.
